A two-part unsent micro-confession for make.fun's "Unsent: The Micro-Confession Sprint" bounty. The program writes one confession — a To: line and the unsent message itself, exactly as it was typed — to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The confession

> To: my little brother
> I took the $20 from your birthday card in 2019, told mum the dog ate the envelope, and I've bought you a present every year since to make up for it.

Why this one: it is specific and honest (a $20, a birthday card, 2019, the dog cover story), the To: line and the message need each other (only a little brother gets a present every year to atone), and the reason it stayed in drafts is felt in the words — years of guilty gift-giving — never stated outright. It reads as typed and never sent, not as a joke written to be sent.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
